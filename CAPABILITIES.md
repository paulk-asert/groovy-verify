# Capabilities

The full inventory of what groovy-verify proves today, by phase — each row a capability, how you author it,
and its status, including the "deferred"/"residual" notes that mark the honest boundaries. For the summary and
the worked examples, see [README.md](README.md#whats-demonstrated); for what the engine *can model* (the in/out
boundary), [FRAGMENT.md](FRAGMENT.md); for the increment history, [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

| **Ticket-lock mutual exclusion (Leino KRML260, bounded process set)** | The safety half of *Modeling Concurrency in Dafny*: a bakery-style mutual-exclusion lock, modelled the paper's way — each atomic event over a system invariant `valid`, **mutual exclusion proved as a consequence** of the invariant, and **every event proved to preserve it**. `Process` is a fixed enum `{A, B}` (Leino sanctions `datatype Process = Agnes | …`), so `cs`/`t` are enum-keyed maps and the `∀p`/`∀p,q` conjuncts expand over the finite domain; events are Model-2 two-state predicates discharged as empty-bodied lemmas (the lattice/monoid-law pattern), sidestepping mutable-map-field framing. | **Ticket-lock bounded bypass (bounded overtaking)** | The liveness result that ships after the full temporal *eventually-eats* hits the trace-composition/induction wall (probed and located in Phase 172: per-step trace reasoning and bounded-`∀` instantiation verify, but induction over a symbolic horizon and chaining implication-guarded transitions do not).
